Notion Integration Credentials

How to get this token?
Step 1: Create a connection
  1. Go to Notion Integrations.
  2. Click New connection.
  3. Name it (e.g. "Super Productivity Sync").
  4. For Authentication method, select Access token.
  5. Click Create connection and copy the token.
Step 2: Share your Database
  1. Go to the Notion database you want to sync.
  2. Click the three dots "..." in the top right.
  3. Click "Connect to" (or "Add connections") and select your connection.
  4. Confirm to give the connection access to your database.
